## Why did you choose software development?

I wanted to pursue a career where I could create something myself. What I particularly enjoy is turning an idea into a working application while combining technical and creative aspects. During my retraining, developing my first applications quickly showed me that I had chosen the right path.

![Divider](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TranceMeli/ColorPalette/main/divider.svg)

## What do you enjoy most about software development?

What motivates me most is the result: an idea becomes something that actually works and can be used. For me, code is more of a tool than the actual goal. I particularly enjoy designing frontends and seeing the direct result in the browser.

![Divider](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TranceMeli/ColorPalette/main/divider.svg)

## Which technologies do you feel most comfortable with?

Python is one of my preferred programming languages. I particularly enjoy working with Vue.js and Django, but I have also discovered ASP.NET Core for myself. JavaScript, CSS and databases are also part of my existing experience.

I am not tied to a specific technology. What matters to me is using the right tool to develop a good solution.

![Divider](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TranceMeli/ColorPalette/main/divider.svg)

## How do you learn new technologies?

I learn best by trying things out directly. Small examples help me get started. I then modify them myself and test different approaches until I understand how something works.

My own projects are particularly helpful because I can apply what I have learned directly.

![Divider](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TranceMeli/ColorPalette/main/divider.svg)

## How do you deal with technical problems?

First, I try to understand a problem myself and explore different possible solutions. I have already encountered several challenges, particularly when dealing with communication between frontend and backend.

When I cannot make further progress, I ask for support. I also use AI tools as a sparring partner to find possible solutions and discover new approaches.

![Divider](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TranceMeli/ColorPalette/main/divider.svg)

## What do you bring to a team?

I am rather quiet and reserved, but helpful and easygoing. I enjoy working independently, but I also value teamwork.

Different perspectives can be helpful when you have become too focused on one particular approach. I also like bringing a little humor into the everyday work environment.

![Divider](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TranceMeli/ColorPalette/main/divider.svg)

## Why do you want to develop towards Full Stack in the long term?

I particularly enjoy frontend development. Through my own projects, however, I have increasingly developed an interest in backend development as well.

What interests me most today is the interaction between both areas. I therefore want to develop a broad skill set and eventually be able to build applications as comprehensively as possible myself.
